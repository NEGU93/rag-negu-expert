Summary of raw file
- Submitted to HAL: 7 Sep 2022 (HAL deposit metadata included in PDF header)
- Contains full paper text (methods, experiments, figures, tables) comparing Complex-Valued Neural Networks (CVNN) and Real-Valued Neural Networks (RVNN) on the Oberpfaffenhofen PolSAR dataset.

Primary contents extracted for wiki ingestion:
- Extended definition of an "equivalent-RVNN" (real-valued network equivalent in number of real trainable parameters while maintaining aspect ratios) including convolutional layers (extension of methods from MLSP 2021 work).
- Experiments comparing CV-MLP, CV-CNN, CV-FCNN and their equivalent RV counterparts (using the proposed ratio-tp technique) on PolSAR semantic segmentation (Oberpfaffenhofen dataset).
- Statistical evaluation over Monte-Carlo trials (50 trials per model) reporting Overall Accuracy (OA) and Average Accuracy (AA); median/mean/confidence intervals reported. Main result: CV-FCNN achieved OA 98.55% and AA 98.14%; overall CVNNs outperform equivalent RVNNs across architectures.

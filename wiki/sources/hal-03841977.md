Summary
-------
This conference paper compares Complex-Valued Fully Convolutional Neural Networks (CV-FCNN) against real-valued equivalents (RV-FCNN) for pixel-wise segmentation of Polarimetric SAR (PolSAR) images. Two input polarimetric representations are tested: the Pauli vector (raw 3-D complex scattering vector) and the (averaged) Hermitian coherency matrix (reshaped non-redundant elements). Experiments use the San Francisco AIRSAR PolSAR dataset (five classes: Mountain, Water, Urban, Vegetation, Bare Soil).

Key points
---------
- Motivation: coherency matrix T is ubiquitous for PolSAR CVNN inputs but averaging (used to form T) reduces spatial resolution and mixes neighboring pixels, which is undesirable for pixel-wise segmentation. Pauli vector k preserves per-pixel complex information.
- Architectures: implemented CV-FCNN (complex U-Net style FCNN) and an equivalent-capacity RV-FCNN (trainable-parameter equivalent as in [12]). Complex activations: Type-A elementwise ReLU (CReLU), complex batch-norm, complex max-pooling (based on magnitude) and complex max-unpooling. Categorical cross-entropy computed by averaging real/imag parts' losses; Adam optimizer (lr=0.01).
- Dataset & protocol: San Francisco AIRSAR (PolSF). Sliding-window patches 128x128, 80% train / 10% val / 10% test (patches generated as in [7]). Each experiment repeated 50 times with different random splits; 400 epochs per training. Statistical analysis uses medians and confidence intervals (median-interval and mean 99% CI).

Results (test set, Table 1 reported)
----------------------------------
Median Average Accuracy (AA) and Overall Accuracy (OA) reported for four conditions (CV-FCNN / RV-FCNN) × (Pauli / Coherency):
- CV-FCNN Pauli: AA median 98.00 ± 0.27; OA median 99.64 ± 0.01
- CV-FCNN Coh:  AA median 96.80 ± 0.25; OA median 99.45 ± 0.02
- RV-FCNN Pauli: AA median 96.75 ± 0.32; OA median 99.40 ± 0.02
- RV-FCNN Coh:  AA median 95.20 ± 0.44; OA median 99.19 ± 0.03

Main findings
-------------
- Using the Pauli vector as input increases segmentation performance (higher AA, slightly higher OA and lower variance) for both complex and real architectures compared to the coherency matrix input.
- CV-FCNN converges faster and attains higher median AA/OA than its RV-FCNN equivalent on this dataset; statistical tests on medians indicate significance in many comparisons (median intervals non-overlapping implies 95% confidence of difference).
- The coherency matrix's local averaging reduces spatial detail, making it suboptimal for pixel-wise segmentation despite its popularity in PolSAR processing.
- The authors recommend favoring Pauli vector representation when available, and report that the Pauli representation also reduces memory footprint (3 complex channels vs 6 non-redundant real values from T).

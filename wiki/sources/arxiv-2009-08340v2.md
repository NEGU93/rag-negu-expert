Title: Complex-Valued vs. Real-Valued Neural Networks for Classification Perspectives: An Example on Non-Circular Data

Authors: J. A. Barrachina, C. Ren, C. Morisseau, G. Vieillard, J.-P. Ovarlez

arXiv: 2009.08340v2 [stat.ML], revised 13 Apr 2021

Local raw file: raw/publications/2009.08340v2.pdf

Abstract (short): The paper studies the potential advantages of Complex-Valued Neural Networks (CVNNs) over Real-Valued Neural Networks (RVNNs) for classification on complex-valued datasets exhibiting non-circular statistics (dependence or unequal variance between real and imaginary parts). The authors compare fully-connected feed-forward CVNNs with equivalent RVNNs across architectures and dataset types, show CVNNs generally achieve higher median/mean accuracy and lower variance, and release a TensorFlow-backed Python library to implement CVNNs.

Extracted metadata / notable points

- Focus: fully-connected feed-forward CVNNs vs equivalent RVNNs for classification on synthetic non-circular complex datasets.
- Mathematical tools: Wirtinger calculus for gradients in complex domain; discussion of circularity/pseudo-variance τ_Z and circularity quotient ̺_Z.
- Activation: Type-A complex activations (elementwise application of ReLU to real & imaginary parts) used for hidden layers; softmax output for classification probabilities.
- Architectures tested: 1 hidden layer (64 units) and 2 hidden layers (100, 40).
- Regularization: dropout (0.5) studied; CVNNs less prone to overfitting than RVNNs.
- Code: references a GitHub repository (Barrachina 2019) implementing CVNNs with TensorFlow as back-end.

Repository referenced in paper: Barrachina, J. A. 2019. Complex-Valued Neural Networks (CVNN). https://github.com/NEGU93/cvnn

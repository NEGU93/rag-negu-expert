- Title: THEORY AND IMPLEMENTATION OF COMPLEX-VALUED NEURAL NETWORKS

Summary of contents (raw notes)

- Detailed tutorial on CVNN theory (Wirtinger calculus, complex backpropagation) and practical implementation notes.
- Library: "cvnn" toolbox implemented in Python on top of TensorFlow (development started 2019); distributed via PIP/Anaconda/Zenodo/GitHub and documented on ReadTheDocs.
- Modules implemented and described: Complex layers (ComplexConv2D, ComplexDense, ComplexInput, ComplexFlatten, ComplexPooling variants including ComplexMaxPooling2DWithArgmax + ComplexUnPooling2D), ComplexBatchNormalization (treating complex as 2D vector + covariances), ComplexDropout, upsampling (nearest/bilinear), Complex transposed convolution, Complex activation families (cartesian / polar / ETFs / modReLU / zReLU / cardioid / MVN), loss functions for complex outputs (including complex average cross-entropy), complex initializers (ComplexGlorotUniform/Normal, ComplexHe variants) and experiments on initialization.
- Experiments: signal classification (chirp, PSK, QAM, noise) with Hilbert transform to produce analytic signals; experiments showing correct complex-aware Glorot matters; comparison CV-MLP vs RV-MLP with reported improvement (~4%) for CVNN on some settings but authors caution architectures not strictly equivalent.

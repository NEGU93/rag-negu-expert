Title: Foro Estratégico — "La Sociedad y la Economía del Conocimiento en la Argentina del siglo 21" (invitation)

Summary: Invitation (29 Apr 2013, Buenos Aires) addressed to Jose Agustín Barrachina to participate as "Líder Sectorial Universitario" in a Jornada organized by Foro Estratégico para el Desarrollo Nacional.

Details
- Date (on letter): 29 de Abril de 2013
- Location (letter header): Mansilla 2686, Cuerpo "A" 2º Piso "5" - C1425BPD Ciudad de Buenos Aires, República Argentina
- Organizer: FORO ESTRATÉGICO PARA EL DESARROLLO NACIONAL
- Contact: Tel/Fax +54 11 4964 2050 / 0844, email foro@foroestrategico.org.ar, www.foroestrategico.org.ar
- Role invited: "Líder Sectorial Universitario"
- Event name (from letter): "La Sociedad y la Economía del Conocimiento en la Argentina del siglo 21"

Agenda (as described in the invitation)
- Morning: central round/table with four expositores whose reflections will aim to elaborate the essence of competitiveness and keys for socio-productive development.
- Afternoon: three "Mesas Estratégicas" where the Líderes Sectoriales will present concrete experiences on productive innovation, planning, public policies and the entrepreneurial role. Participants expected: professionals, entrepreneurs and Argentine scientists.
- Publication: contents of the Jornada will be edited in a "Libro Blanco" compiling disertations, reflections and proposals.

Signatories (on invitation)
- Dra. Norma Cadoppi — Presidente (Foro Estratégico para el Desarrollo Nacional)
- Dr. Santiago Villa — Director de Vinculación y Desarrollo Tecnológico (CONICET)

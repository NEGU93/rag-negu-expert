Project: ResistorCalculator

Short summary

Final project for the course "Programacion" (ITBA, 2016). A small program that lets the user construct a circuit of resistors and computes its Thevenin equivalent.

Details

- Purpose: educational/course project to practice programming and circuit computation.
- Main feature: build resistor circuits and calculate Thevenin equivalent (voltage and resistance implied).
- Demo: Watch the demonstration video on YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=30qZcaUGGkQ
